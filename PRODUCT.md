## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite and React JSX, explicitly requested by the user.

## Product Purpose

A complete architecture and structural design portfolio website following the supplied hand-drawn flow.

## Users

Inferred audience: prospective clients, architects, contractors, and design collaborators exploring the practice and its work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Home with featured project slideshow, practice statistics, services, collaborators, and news. People, project archive with residential/commercial/cultural/institutional categories, contact, and individual project pages with photographs, drawings, sketches, name, description, location, scope, year, status, design partners, area, and awards.

The main navigation includes People, Expertise, Projects, and Contact. Expertise is a dedicated route with the four sample service offerings and enquiry links.

Its sample statistics use the existing illustrative figures: 12+ years of practice, 80+ projects, 16+ cities, and 4+ service areas.

The Expertise route also includes the existing sample journal articles as studio news and the existing sample collaborator names as its clients/collaborators section.

## Brand Commitments

Latest user direction (2026-09-10): the landing page must not scroll. The carousel occupies the entire screen; the navbar overlays the top of the images instead of reserving a separate row. This supersedes the original home page section flow; interior pages remain available.

User approved SPECS as the studio name and sample content. The provided sketch governs information architecture.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the user-provided sketch. All project details, personnel, collaborators, metrics, contact details, and stock photography are demonstration material, not claims about an actual firm. No contact backend or deployment account was supplied.
